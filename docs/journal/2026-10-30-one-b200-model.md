# A model for one B200

**Status:** OPEN. Qwen3.8-Flash-Next NVFP4 chosen and downloaded; validation pending.

## Question
Single B200s start within minutes; pairs queued for 1.5 to 2 h. Which model fits one B200 and sits between Qwen3.8-27B and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash in quality?

## Candidate
Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (2026-08-24): a 125B MoE with 6B active per token, plus a 51B n-gram embedding table and a 4B MTP drafter. The vendor-reported DeepSWE score is 58.7, against 42.2 for Qwen3.8-27B and 77.0 for DeepSeek. The weights are under the Qwen Community License; only Model-as-a-Service businesses need a separate license.

| Variant | Size | Fits 1×B200 (178 GiB) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen FP8 | 186 GB | no: the recipe asks for 250 GB | official |
| Inferact NVFP4 | 183 GB | the recipe runs it on one GB200 (173 GiB) | **picked**: the vLLM recipe's default, Qwen's license |
| NVIDIA NVFP4 | 133 GB | likely | fallback; NVIDIA Open Model License |

vLLM offloads the n-gram table to host memory by default on NVIDIA GPUs, which is what makes a single GPU work.

## Hypothesis (written before the job ran)
- It fits one B200 with the recipe's flags: MTP with 3 draft tokens, FP8 KV cache, text only, and at most 32 sequences.
- Ready within 15 min on the first start.
- 150 to 300 tok/s single-stream; the bug-fix task in under 5 s.
